# Security Policy for Watts Creates Learning

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

At Watts Creates Learning, we take security seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability in our website or services, please report it to us responsibly.

### How to Report

- **Email**: security@wattscreates.com
- **Subject**: Security Vulnerability Report - Watts Creates Learning

### What to Include

1. Description of the vulnerability
2. Steps to reproduce the issue
3. Potential impact assessment
4. Your contact information

### Our Commitment

- We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours
- We will provide regular updates on our investigation
- We will credit security researchers who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities

### Scope

This security policy covers:
- wattscreates.com and all subdomains
- Watts Creates Learning web applications
- Related infrastructure and services

## Watts Creates Learning - Professional Development Security

We are committed to maintaining the highest security standards for our professional development, consulting, and education services. Our security measures protect:

- Client information and privacy
- Workshop and training materials
- Educational resources and content
- Contact and communication data

For questions about our security practices, contact us at create@wattscreates.com.
