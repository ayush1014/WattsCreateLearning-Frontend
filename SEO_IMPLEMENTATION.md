### 1. Technical SEO
- ✅ Optimized HTML meta tags (title, description, keywords)

- ✅ Structured data (JSON-LD) for Organization, Website, and Educational Organization
- ✅ XML sitemap (sitemap.xml) 
- ✅ Robots.txt optimization
- ✅ Canonical URLs implementation
- ✅ Mobile-responsive design maintained
- ✅ Fast loading times with React optimization

### 2. On-Page SEO
- ✅ Strategic keyword placement in titles, headers, and content
- ✅ Page-specific SEO optimization for all main pages (Home, About, Services, Contact)
- ✅ SEO-friendly URLs structure
- ✅ Image optimization with proper alt tags
- ✅ Internal linking structure

### 3. Content SEO
- ✅ High-quality, relevant content about professional development
- ✅ Long-tail keyword targeting
- ✅ FAQ sections and detailed service descriptions
- ✅ Local SEO optimization for US market

### 4. Schema Markup
- ✅ Organization schema for business information
